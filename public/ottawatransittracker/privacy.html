Ottawa Transit Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026. Describes the private Reddit/Devvit app and the conditional features below.

Project identity and contact

Ottawa Transit Tracker is an independent project maintained by Malikah Bain. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by OC Transpo, the City of Ottawa, Environment and Climate Change Canada, the Government of Canada, or the Government of Ontario. For privacy questions, contact malikahbain@gmail.com and identify Ottawa Transit Tracker in the subject. Please do not send credentials or precise travel histories.

Public transit information

The app uses bundled static GTFS schedules, stops, routes and geometry, and GTFS-Realtime VehiclePositions and TripUpdates from City of Ottawa / OC Transpo public transit data services when access is available. These include operational vehicle/trip/stop identifiers, bus coordinates and timestamps, predictions, explicit service cancellations, and source references. A bus coordinate is an operational transit observation, not your device location.

The transit collector fetches whole-network feeds on the app server and filters them inside the app. It sends no Reddit posts, comments, usernames, user IDs, votes, or rider-selected stops to OC Transpo. No device GPS is required. Available routes and schedules depend on the bundled static snapshot and the app version.

Your use of the app

You manually choose a route, direction and stop, or view public transit locations on the map. The app does not request device GPS or build a stored personal travel history. Language and display preferences use local browser storage. Switching between inline and expanded Devvit views can also save the current route/direction/stop selection locally for a brief handoff. The app stops using that handoff after one minute; a record can remain on the device until it is removed, overwritten, or browser storage is cleared.

Selections and public map context may be sent to the app's own server to return relevant transit, weather, traffic or camera information. They are not written to a personal travel-history database. Upstream requests use the public-data flows described below, rather than sending your selections or identity to the providers.

Reddit/Devvit supplies runtime context needed to run the app. Moderator-only diagnostics use the moderator's Reddit username to check authorization within Reddit. The transit collector does not persist that username, Reddit user content, or individual rider selections. Collector metadata includes the installation's subreddit identifier; this is operational app context, not an individual rider profile.

Weather — Environment and Climate Change Canada

When weather is enabled, Ottawa Transit Tracker may retrieve public weather observations, station metadata and official weather alerts from Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC), through api.weather.gc.ca. This integration is implemented locally; Reddit-hosted access remains subject to HTTP-domain approval. Its presence in local code does not mean it is enabled in the hosted app.

Weather lookup uses public route, map viewport, stop or bus coordinates to select relevant station observations and alerts inside the app. It does not use device GPS or send a precise viewer location to ECCC. The app server retrieves shared Ottawa-region data, rather than making an upstream request for each viewer's map selection. No Reddit username or Reddit user ID is intentionally sent to ECCC. Ordinary server/network metadata, such as the app server's connection address, may be visible to ECCC.

Weather data may be cached temporarily to reduce upstream requests. Weather is supplemental and independent of core transit operation; a weather failure does not prevent transit information from working. Weather context does not establish that weather caused a transit delay.

City of Ottawa traffic events

When traffic-event context is enabled, the app may retrieve public City-origin traffic incidents, construction and special events from City of Ottawa Traffic Services through traffic.ottawa.ca. This integration is implemented locally; Reddit-hosted access remains subject to HTTP-domain approval.

Only City-origin records are used in the current implementation. Ministry of Transportation of Ontario (MTO), other third-party and unknown-origin records are excluded. Relevance is calculated inside the app against public route geometry, the map viewport, a selected stop or a bus position. No viewer identity or device GPS is required. Requests are made by the app server; no Reddit username, Reddit user ID, viewer/map coordinates or device GPS location is intentionally sent to the City for event retrieval. Ordinary server/network metadata may be visible to the City.

Traffic records may be cached temporarily. They provide geographic and time context; proximity or overlap with a bus delay does not establish that a traffic event caused that delay.

City of Ottawa traffic cameras

When the nearby traffic-camera feature is enabled, current City-owned traffic-camera images may be retrieved through the app server from traffic.ottawa.ca. MTO cameras and cameras with unknown ownership are excluded. This feature is implemented locally and remains unavailable in the Reddit-hosted app until the required server configuration and Reddit HTTP access are in place.

Camera discovery uses public stop, fresh bus or City traffic-event coordinates already present in the app, with matching performed inside the app. The City's image request contains a camera number, a server-held access certificate and a stable application-instance identifier. That identifier represents the application instance, not the viewer. The request does not intentionally contain your Reddit username, Reddit user ID or device GPS location. The City may receive ordinary server/network metadata as part of the connection.

Frames may be held temporarily in a shared cache and in memory while the gallery is open. They expire after a short period, currently no more than approximately five minutes of usable age measured from retrieval. Retrieval time is not a verified camera capture time. This Live feature does not permanently archive camera images or include them in the separate research archive.

Camera images are contextual only. The app does not analyze them to identify people, infer passenger occupancy or crowding, assess driver behaviour, confirm incidents, or determine causes of delay.

Storage and retention

The Live app uses shared temporary caches for public upstream data to reduce request volume, improve performance and protect third-party services from requests multiplying with each viewer. These operational caches are separate from the research/archive pipeline. Cache retention and the age at which information is usable for display are different; retained data may already be too old to display.

Reddit/Devvit Redis stores current vehicle observations and predictions, feed health, short-lived locks, collection controls, post identifiers and bounded recent collector-run history. Hot transit observations expire in minutes; operational controls and bounded metadata can remain longer. Compact transit summaries and report records may be retained longer for reporting, without storing individual rider selections or a permanent raw-observation archive in Live.

When enabled, weather observations and alerts have temporary cache retention measured in hours, and station metadata may remain for up to seven days. City traffic-event snapshots may remain cached for up to 24 hours, but the app uses them for display only within its shorter freshness limits. Camera catalogue metadata may remain for up to seven days; camera frames have the much shorter usable-age limit described above. These caches do not constitute a permanent weather, traffic-event or camera-image archive.

Live transit operation does not require Supabase or the independent local research collector. Optional legacy Supabase/Postgres validation tooling remains separate. If used, its validation cleanup prunes observations older than 15 minutes and collector-run metadata older than seven days during collection; paused collection does not guarantee immediate deletion. Feed-health rows and provider-managed backups may remain longer according to provider configuration. This legacy validation storage is separate from the local long-term research archive.

Providers and network metadata

Reddit/Devvit hosts the app and processes platform interactions. City of Ottawa / OC Transpo supplies transit data. When the supplemental features are enabled, Environment and Climate Change Canada supplies weather data and City of Ottawa Traffic Services supplies City-origin traffic events and City-owned camera information. Supabase provides storage only for optional legacy validation tooling. GitHub Pages hosts the personal website containing these policy pages; research material may be hosted separately later.

These providers have their own privacy policies and may process network metadata, IP addresses, cookies, account information, security logs or backups when you use their services. Direct visits to provider websites follow those providers' practices. Server-side public-data requests do not intentionally forward your Reddit identity or device GPS. This policy does not claim that network metadata never exists or control all provider retention.

The app uses server-side credentials where needed for transit access, conditional camera access and optional legacy validation storage. The legacy validation path handles public operational transit observations, timestamps, source references and installation metadata, rather than individual rider selections or Reddit user content. The app does not sell personal user data.

Public research releases

The separate local research pipeline archives public transit feeds and static GTFS with provenance for research and reproducibility. It runs independently of the Live Reddit app and does not collect individual rider selections or personal travel histories. Camera frames from the Live feature are not included.

Validated operational transit datasets or aggregate research findings may be published later with quality information and applicable source licences. No public raw dataset release or validated historical reliability report is currently claimed. The weather and traffic-event integrations described here use operational caches; they do not add those observations to the permanent research archive. Any future expansion or release would need its own documented scope and applicable source rights.

Security and accuracy

The project uses safeguards including server-held encrypted credentials, fixed permitted hosts, validated app-server requests and restricted storage access. Camera credentials and authenticated image URLs are not returned to the viewer. No system can guarantee perfect security. Public transit, weather, traffic and camera information can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable; predictions remain projected, and derived stop events may be inferred. This app is informational and research-oriented, without a guarantee of exact arrivals.
