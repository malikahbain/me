How the current app works

You manually choose a route, direction and stop, or view public transit locations on the map. No device GPS is required. The app uses bundled City of Ottawa / OC Transpo static GTFS schedules and, when access is available, GTFS-Realtime VehiclePositions and TripUpdates fetched by the app server. Available routes and schedules depend on the bundled snapshot and app version. External access can be unavailable, and schedules can remain available when realtime data is not.

TripUpdates are predictions, not proof of arrival. Confirmed status requires sufficiently reliable observed or validated reconstructed evidence. Reconstructed events may be inferred and carry uncertainty. A missing observation is not a cancellation; an elapsed scheduled time is not an actual arrival. Stale or unavailable vehicle positions are hidden.

Weather, City traffic events and City-owned traffic cameras are supplemental integrations implemented locally. Their availability in a Reddit-hosted version depends on required HTTP-domain approval and configuration. These terms describe their use when enabled, without representing them as already enabled in the hosted app.

The app displays information obtained from public and third-party sources, including transit and, when enabled, weather and traffic information. The project does not control provider uptime, feed completeness, update timing, accuracy, omissions or changes to provider services. Feeds, schedules, positions, predictions, images and derived labels can be delayed, incomplete, incorrect or unavailable.

The app does not guarantee a particular bus arrival, departure, route assignment, update interval or uninterrupted availability. It is provided for informational and research use. Consult official OC Transpo information for service changes and make travel decisions using the available circumstances, not this app alone. Weather, traffic-event and camera services can fail independently; their failure does not mean transit data itself is unavailable.

Weather context

When weather is enabled, the app may display public observations, station metadata and official alerts from Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC). Weather is contextual, may be delayed or unavailable, and is subject to provider updates. Observations are station-based and may not reflect street-level conditions at a selected stop, bus or map location. Geographic or temporal overlap does not establish that weather caused a transit delay.

Ottawa Transit Tracker is not an emergency weather-warning service and does not guarantee delivery of every current alert. Use official ECCC weather information for current conditions and official warnings, and follow official emergency guidance where appropriate.

City of Ottawa traffic events

When traffic-event context is enabled, the app may display public City-origin reports of incidents, construction and special events. The current implementation excludes MTO, other third-party and unknown-origin records. Reports and their schedules can be incomplete, delayed or unavailable; proximity and temporal overlap provide context only.

The app must not be interpreted as asserting that a particular traffic event caused a bus delay. A nearby report does not establish that a route or bus was affected, and an event's reported status is not independent verification of conditions on the road.

City of Ottawa traffic cameras

When the nearby traffic-camera feature is enabled, images originate from City-owned traffic cameras and are retrieved through the app server. MTO cameras and unknown owners are excluded. Availability and freshness are not guaranteed; images may be delayed, stale or unavailable. The displayed retrieval age is not a verified capture time. Temporarily cached frames are usable for no more than approximately five minutes after retrieval and are not permanently archived by this Live feature.

Camera proximity does not guarantee that the selected stop, bus or traffic event is visible. Images provide context only and should not be interpreted as verification that an incident occurred. The app does not use imagery to identify people, infer passenger occupancy or crowding, assess drivers, or determine causes of delay.

Current storage and separate research

The Live app uses Redis for temporary public-data caches, operational state and compact transit reporting summaries. It does not depend on Supabase or the independent research collector. Optional legacy validation storage and the separate local transit archive are described in the Privacy Policy. Longer-lived compact reporting records are separate from temporary raw-feed caches.

No public research dataset release or validated historical reliability report is represented as already available. Operational transit research releases may be published later with quality checks, provenance and applicable licensing information. Live weather and traffic-event caches do not add those observations to the permanent research archive, and Live camera frames are not permanently archived or included in research releases.
