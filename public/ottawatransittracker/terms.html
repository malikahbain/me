Ottawa Transit Tracker — Terms & Conditions

Effective date: October 5, 2026. Applies to the current private Devvit playtest.

Project and scope

Ottawa Transit Tracker is an independent transit information and research project maintained by Malikah Bain. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OC Transpo, the City of Ottawa, Reddit, or Supabase. The current app is a private beta installed in r/ottawatransittrac_dev. It is not an official transit service or a journey planner.

How the current app works

You manually choose a route, direction, and stop. The app uses City of Ottawa / OC Transpo static GTFS schedules and, when access is available, GTFS-Realtime VehiclePositions and TripUpdates. Current static matching covers six test routes; it is not complete network coverage. External access may be pending, and schedules can remain available when realtime data is not.

TripUpdates are predictions, not proof of arrival. Confirmed status requires sufficiently reliable observed or validated reconstructed evidence. Reconstructed events may be inferred and carry uncertainty. A missing observation is not a cancellation; an elapsed scheduled time is not an actual arrival. Stale or unavailable vehicle positions are hidden.

Accuracy and availability

Feeds, schedules, positions, predictions, and derived labels can be delayed, incomplete, incorrect, or unavailable. The app does not guarantee a particular bus arrival, departure, route assignment, update interval, or uninterrupted availability. It is provided for informational and research use. Consult official OC Transpo information for service changes and make travel decisions using the available circumstances, not this app alone.

Acceptable use and source rights

Use the app consistently with Reddit's terms and rules. Do not attempt to obtain credentials, bypass access controls, or disrupt the app or its providers. Source transit data remains subject to the applicable City of Ottawa / OC Transpo terms and licences; displaying it here does not grant additional rights or official endorsement. Future research releases will identify applicable licences and methodology separately.

Current storage and future research

Devvit Redis holds current operational state. Supabase/Postgres holds short-lived validation observations and collector metadata. Historical automatic report publishing is disabled, and no public research dataset release is represented as already available. Aggregated or operational transit research releases may be published later, with quality checks, provenance, and licensing information. Traffic/open-data context from the City of Ottawa and public weather context such as Environment Canada are planned, not connected features of this playtest.
